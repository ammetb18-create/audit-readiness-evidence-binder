# Remediation Plan

## Purpose

This remediation plan prioritizes corrective actions for the audit readiness gaps identified in the gap analysis.

From a GRC analyst perspective, a remediation plan helps assign ownership, define timelines, track corrective actions, and support management visibility into cybersecurity and compliance improvement efforts.

## Remediation Priority Model

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| High | Should be addressed first because the gap creates significant cybersecurity, compliance, or audit readiness risk. |
| Medium | Should be addressed after high-priority items or as part of ongoing governance improvement. |
| Low | Should be addressed as part of routine documentation or process maturity efforts. |

## 0–30 Day Remediation Focus

The first 30 days should focus on high-priority identity and access controls.

| Focus Area | Actions |
|---|---|
| Access Reviews | Define review scope, export user access lists, assign reviewers, document sign-off. |
| MFA Evidence | Generate MFA coverage report, identify exceptions, document approval for exceptions. |
| Privileged Access | Create privileged user inventory and validate administrative access. |

## 31–60 Day Remediation Focus

The next 30 days should focus on vendor risk, offboarding, logging, and incident response readiness.

| Focus Area | Actions |
|---|---|
| Offboarding | Standardize checklist and document access removal evidence. |
| Vendor Risk | Collect security questionnaires, SOC 2 reports, and vendor risk ratings. |
| Logging | Confirm log retention expectations and collect sample log evidence. |
| Incident Response | Conduct tabletop exercise and document lessons learned. |

## 61–90 Day Remediation Focus

The final phase should focus on governance, evidence management, and recurring compliance processes.

| Focus Area | Actions |
|---|---|
| Policy Management | Create policy inventory and confirm review dates. |
| Evidence Management | Maintain centralized evidence tracker and repository structure. |
| Remediation Tracking | Track corrective actions to closure with owners and due dates. |
| Executive Reporting | Summarize open gaps, remediation progress, and residual risk. |

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Percentage of access reviews completed | Measures identity governance progress. |
| MFA coverage percentage | Measures authentication control readiness. |
| Number of privileged accounts reviewed | Measures privileged access governance. |
| Percentage of critical vendors reviewed | Measures third-party risk management progress. |
| Incident response tabletop completed | Measures incident response readiness. |
| Evidence tracker completion rate | Measures audit evidence organization. |
| Number of remediation items closed | Measures corrective action progress. |

## Management Reporting Summary

Management should receive periodic updates that include:

- Open gaps
- High-priority remediation items
- Overdue tasks
- Control owner status
- Evidence completion rate
- Residual risk concerns
- Upcoming audit readiness milestones

## GRC Analyst Takeaway

This remediation plan demonstrates how a GRC analyst converts audit readiness gaps into structured corrective actions.

The plan supports:

- Control owner accountability
- Audit readiness improvement
- Risk-based prioritization
- Executive reporting
- Compliance documentation
- Continuous governance maturity
