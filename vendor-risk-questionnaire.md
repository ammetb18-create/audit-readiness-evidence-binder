# Vendor Risk Questionnaire

## Purpose

This Vendor Risk Questionnaire is designed to support third-party cybersecurity and compliance review activities.

From a GRC analyst perspective, vendor risk questionnaires help determine whether third-party providers maintain appropriate security, privacy, operational, and compliance controls before they are approved or renewed.

## Data Access and Privacy

| # | Question | Response / Evidence Needed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does the vendor process, store, or transmit sensitive company or customer data? | Describe data types and provide supporting documentation. |
| 2 | What categories of data are shared with the vendor? | Personal data, financial data, authentication data, business data, logs, etc. |
| 3 | Where is the data stored or processed? | Provide geographic location or hosting environment. |
| 4 | Does the vendor have a documented data retention and deletion process? | Provide policy or procedure. |

## Risk Rating Guidance

| Rating | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Low | Vendor has limited access to sensitive data and low business impact. |
| Medium | Vendor supports important business processes or has moderate access to sensitive data. |
| High | Vendor processes sensitive customer, financial, authentication, or regulated data, or supports critical operations. |

## GRC Analyst Takeaway

A vendor risk questionnaire helps a GRC analyst evaluate third-party cybersecurity risk before sensitive data or critical processes are shared with an external provider.

This type of documentation supports:

- Third-party risk management
- Audit readiness
- Compliance documentation
- Contract security review
- Incident response expectations
- Data protection oversight
- Vendor lifecycle governance
