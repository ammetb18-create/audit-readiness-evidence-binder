# Company Scenario

## Purpose

This section defines the business scenario for the Audit Readiness Evidence Binder. The purpose is to create a realistic cybersecurity GRC environment where audit evidence, control ownership, policies, and remediation activities must be organized.

This scenario is designed to demonstrate practical audit readiness skills from the perspective of a cybersecurity GRC analyst.

## Business Environment

The company in this case study is a growing technology organization that provides a digital platform for customers and business users. The organization manages sensitive business information, user accounts, internal systems, vendor-supported services, and cloud-based workflows.

As the company grows, leadership wants to improve cybersecurity governance and prepare for a formal compliance or security review.

## Audit Readiness Context

The organization needs to demonstrate that key cybersecurity controls are documented, assigned to owners, supported by evidence, and reviewed on a recurring basis.

The audit readiness effort focuses on the following control areas:

- Access control
- Multi-factor authentication
- Password security
- Vendor risk management
- Security policies
- Security monitoring
- Incident response readiness
- Evidence collection
- Remediation tracking
- Control ownership

## Business Need

The company wants to reduce cybersecurity and compliance risk by organizing its control documentation and preparing evidence that could support an internal audit, customer security review, or external compliance assessment.

The main business needs are:

- Improve control visibility
- Identify missing evidence
- Assign control owners
- Standardize documentation
- Track remediation progress
- Support audit and compliance requests
- Improve executive visibility into cybersecurity readiness

## Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role in Audit Readiness |
|---|---|
| Executive Leadership | Reviews cybersecurity risk, audit readiness status, and remediation priorities. |
| GRC / Compliance Team | Coordinates evidence collection, control documentation, gap analysis, and remediation tracking. |
| IT / Security Team | Provides technical evidence, access reports, MFA configuration details, logs, and security procedures. |
| HR Team | Supports employee onboarding, offboarding, and access lifecycle evidence. |
| Vendor Management Team | Provides vendor due diligence documentation and third-party risk evidence. |
| Control Owners | Own specific controls and provide supporting documentation or evidence. |

## Audit Readiness Challenges

The company faces common audit readiness challenges, including:

- Evidence is stored in different locations
- Control owners are not clearly assigned
- Access reviews are not consistently documented
- Vendor security reviews are incomplete
- Policies exist but may not be recently reviewed
- Incident response procedures may not be tested
- Remediation tasks are not centrally tracked
- Executive reporting is limited

## Project Scope

This binder focuses on building a practical documentation structure to support audit readiness.

The project includes:

- Control inventory
- Evidence request tracker
- Access control policy
- MFA and password policy
- Vendor risk questionnaire
- Audit gap analysis
- Remediation plan
- Executive summary

## GRC Analyst Takeaway

This scenario demonstrates how a cybersecurity GRC analyst supports audit readiness by organizing controls, collecting evidence, identifying gaps, assigning ownership, and translating compliance needs into clear documentation and remediation actions.
