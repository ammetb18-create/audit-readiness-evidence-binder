About the Project

This project presents a practical evidence binder for a growing technology company preparing for a cybersecurity compliance review, customer security assessment, or internal audit.

Project Objective

The objective is to demonstrate how a GRC analyst organizes cybersecurity control evidence, identifies documentation gaps, assigns control ownership, and prepares a company for audit or compliance review.

Business Value

  • Improves audit readiness by organizing control evidence and documentation.
  • Supports access control governance and recurring review processes.
  • Identifies evidence gaps before audits or customer security reviews.
  • Strengthens vendor risk management and third-party security review.
  • Creates accountability through control owners, due dates, and remediation tracking.
  • Helps translate cybersecurity controls into business-readable documentation.
